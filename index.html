Privacy Policy

How information is handled when you play our Halloween matching game.

Effective and last updated: October 28, 2026 · Android package: com.gameworld.pumpkin

At a glance

You can play without creating an account. Game progress and settings are stored on your device. The app includes advertising technology for optional rewarded videos; advertising services may process device, network and ad-interaction information when active. This policy explains that processing and your choices.

1. Who we are and what this policy covers

This policy applies to Pumpkin Mischief (the “app”), published by Dingsheng Network Technology Limited (“we”, “us” or “our”). It covers the Android app and this privacy policy page.

The game runs from files included in the app. It does not require a player account, social login or a cloud save service operated by us.

2. Information and purposes

Local game information

The game stores progress, round results, preferences and a randomly generated local player identifier in the app’s private storage, including WebView storage. This supports saved progress and game settings. The local identifier is not a registered online account. Our game code does not upload this local save information to a game backend.

Motion controls

The app uses accelerometer readings while it is active to move or stir game objects. Our motion-control code uses these readings on the device and does not record a motion history or send the readings to our servers or advertising partners.

Advertising and technical information

When advertising services are active, the mediation provider and participating ad networks may collect or receive advertising or app/device identifiers, IP address, approximate location inferred from IP address, device model, operating system and app versions, language, network information, ad impressions, video views, clicks, reward events and diagnostic or performance information. The data processed depends on the provider, device settings, region and privacy choices.

These services use information to deliver and measure ads, limit repeated ads, provide rewards, detect invalid traffic and fraud, troubleshoot errors and operate or improve their advertising services. Non-personalized advertising can still involve technical information and identifiers; it does not mean that no data is processed.

Information you send us

If you contact us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include so we can respond, investigate a problem or handle a privacy request. Please avoid sending passwords, verification codes or other unnecessary sensitive information.

The game does not ask you to provide contacts, photos, microphone recordings, financial information or precise GPS location to play.

4. Your choices and rights

  • Advertising privacy: open the app’s options menu and choose “Privacy options” to review available consent choices. The form is supplied by the consent service and may not be required or available in every region. You can also use Android’s advertising privacy settings to reset or delete the advertising ID or change available ad controls.
  • Local data: clear the app’s storage in Android Settings or uninstall the app to remove its local saves and settings. This resets game progress.
  • Privacy requests: depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or obtain a copy of personal information, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent, or opt out of sale, sharing or targeted advertising. Contact us using the address below. We will handle requests under applicable law and may ask for information needed to verify or locate the request.
  • Partner-held information: clearing app storage does not delete information already received by an advertising partner. Use the partner’s privacy controls or contact it through the notices above; you may also contact us for assistance.

Where applicable, processing is based on consent, the need to provide requested app features, legitimate interests such as security and support, or legal obligations. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that was lawful before withdrawal. You may complain to your local data protection authority.

5. Retention and deletion

Local game saves remain on your device until you clear app storage or uninstall the app. We do not operate a server copy of these saves. Support correspondence is kept only as needed to resolve the request and meet applicable legal or security requirements, then deleted when no longer needed. You can request deletion by contacting us.

Advertising providers determine retention of information on their systems under their own policies and legal requirements. We cannot erase their records by resetting your local game data.

6. Security and international processing

Local app data is stored within Android’s app storage protections. We use reasonable measures to protect information we handle, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure. Advertising and hosting providers may process data in countries other than yours, under their published safeguards and applicable law.

7. Children’s privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. A parent or guardian who believes a child has provided personal information can contact us to request review and deletion. Store age ratings describe content suitability and do not replace any applicable privacy requirements.
